Stockout-aware inventory

Walmart M5 data, 30,490 product-store series in 10 stores (2011 to 2016). An empty shelf records zero sales, so standard forecasts read stockouts as low demand and order less. This dashboard shows what that costs and what a stockout-aware reorder rule saves in a one-year simulation.

Why it works

How the numbers were made

  • Stockouts: a run of L zero-sale days is flagged when its probability under the item's normal 56-day sales rate λ, e^(−λL), is below 0.0001. A simulated store with no stockouts was used to estimate that about two-thirds of flags are real, and lost sales were scaled down to match.
  • Simulation: weekly order-up-to replenishment with a 2-day lead time for 29,906 items. Each item's real detected stockout periods were replayed as supply outages that hit every policy equally. Holding cost 25% of price per year; a lost sale costs 30% of price.
  • Policies: fixed safety stock (3 days of demand), forecast-based (95% target from the last 28 days of sales) and stockout-aware (the same, but leaving out days the shelf was empty).
  • All results come from the project's R scripts and are reproducible from the Kaggle M5 Forecasting – Accuracy data.
